RaiLink

Direct seat not available? No problem! Railink stitches together available vacancies from different seats and time slots to build a complete journey plan.

Example: Instead of waiting for a direct seat, you can travel in S2 - 5(Station A -> B) and S4 - 11(Station B -> C) to reach your destination.

Waitlisted? No direct seats available? Stitch available vacancies and finish your journey after chart preparation.
